1. Introduction

Welcome to Aroma Bites.

At Aroma Bites, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information when you visit our website, enquire about our services, purchase a meal subscription or communicate with our team.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

By using our website or placing an order, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • Locality and PIN code

B. Meal Preferences and Dietary Information

To provide personalised meal plans, we may collect information such as:

  • Food preferences (vegetarian, non-vegetarian, etc.)
  • Dietary restrictions and ingredient exclusions
  • Preferred meal timings
  • Fitness or nutrition goals voluntarily shared by you
  • Calorie, protein or macronutrient preferences, where applicable

You should only share information necessary for your meal requirements. We do not intend to collect medical information beyond what is necessary to address a specific dietary request.

C. Order and Subscription Information

  • Selected subscription plan
  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner preferences
  • Order history
  • Subscription duration
  • Pause, cancellation and refund requests
  • Delivery instructions

D. Payment Information

Payments are processed through the payment methods made available on our website.

We may receive transaction references, payment status and other necessary transaction information.

We do not intend to store complete debit card, credit card or banking credentials on our own servers.

E. Technical Information

When you visit our website, we or our service providers may collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Website usage information
  • Cookies and similar technologies, where enabled

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for legitimate business and service-related purposes, including:

  1. Processing meal subscription purchases and orders.
  2. Preparing and delivering your selected meals.
  3. Creating personalised meal plans based on your preferences.
  4. Coordinating delivery schedules and addresses.
  5. Managing subscription pauses, modifications and cancellations.
  6. Processing eligible refund requests.
  7. Responding to customer enquiries and complaints.
  8. Improving food quality and customer experience.
  9. Sending essential order and delivery updates.
  10. Meeting applicable legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations.

We will not use your personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

4. Sharing of Personal Information

Aroma Bites does not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers where necessary to operate our business, including:

  • Delivery personnel and logistics partners.
  • Payment gateway and transaction service providers.
  • Website hosting and technical service providers.
  • Customer communication and support platforms.
  • Professional advisers, auditors and legal authorities where required.

These parties should receive only the information necessary to perform their respective services and must handle it in accordance with applicable contractual and legal obligations.

5. Payment Security

We aim to maintain secure payment practices.

Online transactions may be handled by third-party payment service providers. Their privacy policies and security practices will also apply to the payment information processed through their systems.

• Aroma Bites does not offer automatic recurring subscription deductions under its current one-time payment arrangement.

• Customers must actively place a new order to purchase another subscription.

6. Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality.
  • Remember user preferences.
  • Understand website traffic.
  • Analyse website performance.
  • Improve the browsing experience.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if certain cookies are disabled.

Where required, we will obtain appropriate consent before using non-essential cookies or tracking technologies.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Certain records may be retained for longer periods where required for:

  • Tax and accounting obligations.
  • Legal compliance.
  • Resolving disputes.
  • Preventing fraud.
  • Maintaining transaction records.

When personal information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

8. Data Security

Aroma Bites takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against:

  • Unauthorised access.
  • Accidental disclosure.
  • Data loss.
  • Misuse or alteration.
  • Unauthorised processing.

However, no internet-based platform or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. In the event of a personal data breach, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally permissible.
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing.

  • Seek grievance redressal through our support channels.

Requests can be submitted using the contact details provided below.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 will be followed as and when their respective provisions become applicable.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and subscription services are not intended to independently collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation.

Where information relating to a child is required for a meal order, it should be provided by a parent or lawful guardian, subject to applicable law.
